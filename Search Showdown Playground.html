🧠 Dense Lab: search by meaning

This lab is an illustration (for the real model, open ⚡ Live Search). The positions below are hand-placed to show the idea. The real model (MiniLM) turns every article into 384 numbers learned from data, and compares them in the same way (cosine).

Every headline is a direction on the circle. Headlines with similar meaning point the same way. The pink arrow is your query. Articles are ranked by cosine similarity: the closer the direction, the higher the score. No shared words are needed.

🔀 The Mixer (Reciprocal Rank Fusion)

The hybrid merges two ranked lists by rank, not by score. Each article earns weight ÷ (k + position) from each list. Move articles up or down and watch the merged list change.
